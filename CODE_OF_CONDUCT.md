# Code of conduct

Jupydex aims to be a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while asking for evidence.
- Respect privacy and never repost another person's sensitive data.
- Make room for different experience levels and communication styles.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwelcome attention;
- publishing private information without permission;
- knowingly sharing live credentials or exploit details in public;
- sustained disruption, spam, or bad-faith argument.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the community or project.

Report conduct issues privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile
or the repository's private security-advisory channel when sensitive details
are involved. Do not include unnecessary personal data.
